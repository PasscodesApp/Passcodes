## Our approach

Passcodes is a local-first password manager. Your information is stored on your
device. We do not require an account, and we do not run a service that stores
your passwords. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or
analytics tools.

## Information Passcodes handles

Passcodes handles the information you choose to put into it, such as:

- **Saved logins**: site or app names, usernames, passwords, notes, web
  addresses and the categories you create.
- **Autofill details** (optional): personal details such as your name, email
  addresses, phone numbers, birth date and address, if you add them to use
  with Autofill.
- **App settings and preferences**, such as security options and display choices.

This information stays on your device. The developers do not receive it.

## How your information is used

Your information is used only to provide the features of the app: storing and
showing your passwords, organising them, generating new passwords, importing and
exporting your data, and filling forms and logins on your device when you use
Autofill.

## When information leaves your device

Passcodes only shares information in the following situations:

- **Website icons.** To show a site's icon, the app can request it from an
  icon service. This sends the site's **domain name** (and, as with any
  internet request, your IP address) to the service you select in Settings.
  You can turn this off. Your usernames and passwords are never sent.
- **Exports and backups.** When you export your data, a file is created where
  you choose to save it. Exported files are currently **not encrypted**; anyone
  who can open the file can read it. You are responsible for keeping it safe.
- **Autofill.** If you enable Autofill, Passcodes provides your saved
  information to the app or website you are using, through your device's
  Autofill system.
- **Clipboard and links.** Copying a password places it on your device's
  clipboard, which other apps may be able to read. Opening a link takes you to
  another app or website with its own privacy practices.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

## Third-party services

The optional icon services are operated by third parties (such as Google,
DuckDuckGo or Favicon.im) and are governed by their own privacy policies. The
app stores and distribution platforms you use to get Passcodes also have their
own policies.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including an optional
app lock using your device's biometrics or screen lock, protection against
screenshots in sensitive screens, confirmation before exporting your data, and
turning off device backups of the app's data.

No method of storage is completely secure. Because Passcodes does not encrypt
your data yet, the security of your information also depends on your device:
keep a screen lock enabled, keep your system up to date, and keep control of
your phone. If you find a security problem, please report it privately as
described in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

## Data retention and deletion

Your information is kept on your device until you delete it. Deleting a
password may first move it to a "recently deleted" area, from which you can
restore it or remove it permanently. Uninstalling the app or clearing its
storage removes the data kept inside the app, but not files you exported or
copied elsewhere.

Because we do not hold a copy of your data, we cannot recover it or delete it
on your behalf.

## Your choices

You control your information in the app: you can edit or delete your data,
export it, change security and Autofill settings, turn off website icons, and
disable Autofill in your device settings.

## Children

Passcodes is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates are published in this
repository with a new effective date, and the history of changes is visible
there. If we add features that change how information is handled, such as
accounts, syncing, analytics or encryption, we will update this policy.
